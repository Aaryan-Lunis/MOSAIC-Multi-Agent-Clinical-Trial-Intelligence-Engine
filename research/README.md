# Research

This directory contains the research literature, implementation notes, and supporting references used during the development of **MOSAIC – Multi-Agent Clinical Trial Intelligence Engine**.

The objective of this research library is to document the academic foundation of the project and maintain organized notes on the literature that influenced the system's architecture and implementation.

## Organization

The research is grouped into the following categories:

- Core Clinical Trial Intelligence
- Data Sources and Knowledge
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Medical Retrieval
- Multi-Agent Architecture
- Clinical NLP and Eligibility
- Benchmarks and Evaluation

Each category contains structured notes summarizing the corresponding research papers, along with their relevance to the MOSAIC project.

> **Note:** This repository contains research notes, references, and implementation insights. Copyrighted research papers are not redistributed. Please access papers through their official publisher or DOI.
