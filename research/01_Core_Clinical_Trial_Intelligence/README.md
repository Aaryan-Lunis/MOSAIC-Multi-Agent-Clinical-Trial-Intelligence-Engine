# Core Clinical Trial Intelligence

This category contains the foundational research papers that directly influenced the design of the **MOSAIC – Multi-Agent Clinical Trial Intelligence Engine**.

These studies address the core challenges of clinical trial intelligence, including patient-to-trial matching, eligibility assessment, trial recommendation, intelligent clinical reasoning, and AI-assisted clinical trial design.

## Research Focus

- Patient-to-trial matching
- Eligibility criteria reasoning
- Clinical trial recommendation
- Clinical trial design using LLMs
- Explainable clinical decision support

## Papers

| Paper | Primary Contribution |
|--------|----------------------|
| TrialGPT | End-to-end LLM framework for patient-trial matching |
| TrialMind | Clinical evidence synthesis using LLMs |
| AutoTrial | LLM-assisted clinical trial design |
| Trial2Vec | Representation learning for clinical trial retrieval |
| ClinicalAgent | Multi-agent reasoning for clinical trial workflows |

## Why This Category Matters

This literature forms the core of MOSAIC's intelligence layer. The ideas presented in these papers directly influence the design of the Patient Matching Agent, Eligibility Assessment Agent, Trial Recommendation Agent, and Clinical Reasoning components of the system.
