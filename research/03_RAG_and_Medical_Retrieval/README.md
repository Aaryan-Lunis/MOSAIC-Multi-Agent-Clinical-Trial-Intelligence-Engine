# Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Medical Retrieval

This category contains research papers focused on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), biomedical information retrieval, and evidence-based medical question answering. These approaches enable large language models to retrieve reliable external knowledge before generating responses, improving factual accuracy and reducing hallucinations.

## Research Focus

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Biomedical Information Retrieval
- Medical Question Answering
- Evidence Retrieval
- Clinical Knowledge Integration
- Hybrid Retrieval Systems

## Papers

| Paper | Primary Contribution |
|--------|----------------------|
| MedRAG | Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework for medical question answering |
| i-MedRAG | Iterative RAG for improved medical reasoning and evidence retrieval |
| CliniDigest | Clinical trial information retrieval and summarization using LLMs |

## Why This Category Matters

This category forms the knowledge retrieval layer of MOSAIC. Instead of relying solely on an LLM's internal knowledge, the system retrieves the latest clinical trials and biomedical evidence from trusted sources such as ClinicalTrials.gov and PubMed before generating recommendations. These papers directly influence the Retrieval Agent, Evidence Synthesis Agent, and Clinical Knowledge Retrieval pipeline.
