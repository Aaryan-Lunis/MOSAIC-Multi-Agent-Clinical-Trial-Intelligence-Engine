# Clinical NLP and Eligibility

This category contains research papers focused on clinical natural language processing (Clinical NLP), eligibility criteria extraction, and patient-to-trial matching.

Clinical trial eligibility criteria are often written in unstructured text, making automated matching difficult. The papers in this category explore methods for extracting, interpreting, and structuring eligibility criteria to support intelligent clinical trial matching.

## Research Focus

- Clinical Natural Language Processing (Clinical NLP)
- Eligibility Criteria Extraction
- Patient-Trial Matching
- Medical Text Understanding
- Information Extraction
- Structured Clinical Representation

## Papers

| Paper | Primary Contribution |
|--------|----------------------|
| Criteria2Query 3.0 | Converts free-text eligibility criteria into executable database queries |
| AutoCriteria | Automated extraction and normalization of clinical trial eligibility criteria |
| PRISM | AI-assisted patient recruitment and eligibility screening for clinical trials |

## Why This Category Matters

Eligibility assessment is one of the core components of MOSAIC. The techniques presented in these papers enable the system to understand complex eligibility criteria, compare them with patient information, and generate transparent eligibility decisions. These ideas directly influence the Eligibility Agent, Clinical NLP Pipeline, and Patient Matching Engine.
